---
description: "MCP tool — browser_show"
---

Present the browser to the human — bring the Eos browser panel to the front showing `tabId` (default: your session's active tab). Call it when you WANT the human to look now — a finished site, game, or preview — not on routine navigations (repeat calls within a few seconds are dropped). Read-only: it shows the panel, it does not change the page.
