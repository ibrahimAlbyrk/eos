Recall what Eos learned from earlier work where you are now: the user's corrections, lessons from commands that failed, steps that worked. Notes come ranked, with their id, where they apply and whether they are firm or still a hint. Notes that match your situation already arrive on their own; use this before a step you are unsure of (a push, a release, a migration) or when an error looks familiar. A hint was learned without asking: check it before relying on it.
