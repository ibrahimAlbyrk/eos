---
description: "Tool — no_reply"
---

End your turn without writing anything. Call it alone, as the only thing in your response: the turn ends with it, and nothing written or called beside it is followed up.

When to use:
- The user's reaction only acknowledged something (👌 on a report): they know, and nothing is asked of you.
- Your own reaction already said everything.

When NOT to use:
- The user asked or told you something that needs an answer or work. A reaction that answers your question ("yes, do it") is such a thing: do the work.
