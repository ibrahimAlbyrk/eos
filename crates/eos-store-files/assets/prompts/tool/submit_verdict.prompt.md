---
description: "Tool — submit_verdict"
---

Give your verdict on the result you were asked to check.

- `pass`: true only when every check holds.
- `checks`: one entry per check of the routine: its `key`, `ok`, and a one-sentence `note` on why.
- `mustFix`: when the result fails, exactly what the lead must change, one point each. Empty when it passes.

Call it once, then end your turn.
